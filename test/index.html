Join the Remote Wand test programme

Closed testing on Google Play · Android phones and tablets

Remote Wand is in closed testing before its public release. Google requires at least 12 testers for 14 days; every tester with an Android phone helps. Testers get Remote Wand Pro for free during the test.

  1. Join the tester group with your Google account: groups.google.com/g/krmndigital (one group for all KRMN Digital apps).
  2. Opt in to the test: play.google.com/apps/testing/com.krmndigital.remotewand.
  3. On your phone, open Google Play, search for Remote Wand and install it (it may take a few hours to appear after opting in).
  4. Try it with your TV (Android TV / Google TV, Samsung, LG or Roku over Wi-Fi; any TV with Bluetooth) and, if you like, with a Windows PC using Remote Wand Desktop.
  5. Please stay opted in for at least 14 days, and send feedback to krmndigital@gmail.com: which TV model, what worked, what did not.
