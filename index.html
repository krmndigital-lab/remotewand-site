Remote Wand

Universal TV remote & PC mouse/keyboard for Android · by KRMN Digital

One app on your phone that replaces the TV remote and adds a wireless mouse and keyboard for your computer. Works over Wi-Fi with Android TV / Google TV, Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS) and Roku — nothing to install on the TV — and over Bluetooth with any TV, streaming box or PC that accepts a Bluetooth keyboard. Written in Rust: instant start, tiny download, no accounts, no tracking.

Get it on Google Play Remote Wand Desktop for PC

What it does

  • TV remote over Wi-Fi: D-pad, volume, channels, power, media keys, number pad, colour keys, inputs, app launcher and typing with your phone keyboard.
  • Bluetooth keyboard, mouse and media remote: your phone shows up on the TV or PC like a Bluetooth keyboard. Works with Fire TV, Android TV, Samsung, LG, Windows, macOS and Linux.
  • PC over Wi-Fi (Pro): trackpad, keyboard with special keys, unicode typing, media and volume keys, presentation keys, lock / sleep / shut down — with the free Remote Wand Desktop companion.
  • Touchpad & air mouse: smooth pointer on TVs that support it; point with the phone (Pro).
  • Macros (Pro): one tap for "Movie night": TV on, HDMI 2, volume 15.
  • 10 languages: English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Indonesian.

Free and Pro

Remote Wand is free with ads and one saved device. Remote Wand Pro is a small one-time purchase: no ads, unlimited devices, PC control over Wi-Fi, air mouse, macros and accent themes. No subscription.
